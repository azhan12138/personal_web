# Homepage prototype verdict

## Question

Which information structure best balances student identity, professional credibility, and AI Native exploration?

## Decision

- Variant A (`Editorial Ledger / 编辑档案`) is the selected direction.
- Variants B and C are rejected as primary directions.
- The first section after the hero must be `Education & Academic Foundation`.

## Education-section requirements

- Treat education as a primary recruiting signal, not a footnote in the journey timeline.
- Show both Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Southwestern University of Finance and Economics.
- Include degree, dates, representative coursework, honors, competitions, and current research focus.
- Keep the section résumé-like and easy to scan while preserving Variant A's editorial visual language.
- Do not publish GPA, ranking, course scores, phone number, or other previously excluded metrics.

## Current prototype answer

The page now follows:

`Hero → Education → Current focus → AI Native explorations → Journey → Contact`

The production page should reimplement this validated hierarchy rather than promoting the prototype code directly.
